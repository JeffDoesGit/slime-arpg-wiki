# The soul-keeper

*Built in roadmap items E-5.9, E-5.10 and CT-5.2's first half (2026-11-08 and 2026-11-09); the verbs folded to two, Stage and Render, in E-5.29 (2026-11-12, Jon's playtest note of 2026-11-10). Design rules: GDD 2.6 (stages), 2.8 (spare copies), 4.4 (the two currencies) and 6.3 (the station), decided 2026-11-08 on Jon's rulings; `docs/decisions/D-5.4.md` Part B as amended by its section 15 (shape A, the ledger). Nothing here is provisional. The station stands in the town as a grey block with its own screen; its body, a MetaHuman NPC, is CT-5.2's second half and is not built.*

## The idea

Souls are never lost ([Souls](soul-model.md)): the first copy of a soul you win is the soul itself, the unlock, and no verb anywhere spends it. Every copy past the first is a **spare copy**, and spares are a currency. They buy the permanent things on that soul: its tree regions, fed at a gate on the tree screen ([Soul webs](soul-webs.md), Feeding a region's gate), and its **stages**, bought at the soul-keeper. A spare can also be **rendered** into a white shard for the gem bench. The flow is one way, kill to bench: fragments assemble into a copy, a copy renders into a white shard, and nothing ever returns a copy, a fragment or essence. A duplicate soul is never worthless and never worth more than trash.

Until E-5.29 a spare rendered into **essence** of its soul's type, and essence bought the recurring things (Rethread, the lair altar, a chosen card) and white shards at an exchange. Jon's playtest note of 2026-11-10 folded that away: "for soul-keeper fold render and exchange into just exchange, crafts should be stage and render (where render exchanges 1 soul for 1 white shard)". No verb makes essence now and the Exchange is gone; essence already in a bag stays there and does nothing. What pays Rethread (E-5.11), the soul reagents (E-5.12) and the lair altar (E-5.27) is Jon's pick, not yet made.

## Stages

A soul has a stage, 0 to V. Each stage costs spare copies of that soul: one for I, one for II, two for III, two for IV, three for V, nine copies in all (placeholders in the `DT_SoulKeeper` table). Each stage adds 5 % (a table value) to that soul's own growth values, the health and mana it adds a level; its growth share rides the multiplier whether it is your primary or your secondary. Until 2026-11-14 a stage also added 5 % to the base damage of every one of the soul's moves; Jon replaced that half with ability levels and a main ability you pick (below, E-2.301). The stage is kept on the soul's unlock and never lost; it is never summed into any score. It shows on the soul's orb as a roman numeral in gold at the orb's lower left, across from the level at the lower right, in the soul slots and on the bag cell. A cosmetic soul (the human) has no stage.

A stage also unlocks one dye for the soul's body and a glow for its orb (GDD 2.6); the dye is E-5.28 and is not built.

## Ability levels and the main ability (E-2.301)

Every ability has a level from 1 to 20, and each level makes the ability's own hits 8 % harder than the level before (level 5 is x1.36, level 11 x2.16, level 20 x4.32). Every ability stands at least at its floor: 1, plus one level for every ten character levels (level 1 to 9 is 1, 10 to 19 is 2, 20 is 3, 100 is 11). The basic attack only ever has the floor.

At the keeper you pick one of a staged soul's abilities as its **main** ability. It gains two levels for each stage the soul has (stage II: four levels over the floor). From stage IV you may also pick a **secondary** ability, which gains two levels at stage IV and three at stage V. Neither can be the basic attack, and one ability cannot be both. An ability's level can never pass what your character level allows: level 2 needs character level 2, then 4, 6, 8 and so on up to level 11 at character level 20, then one more level every eight character levels (level 20 at 92). Levels a stage gives above that wait, banked, and arrive as you level: the ability's tooltip then reads "Level 5 (7 at character level 14)". All of these numbers are placeholders in the `DT_AbilityLevels` and `DT_SoulKeeper` tables.

Picking is free and can be changed any time the keeper screen is open, for any soul at stage I or more (the secondary from stage IV). A soul with a stage and no main ability picked gives no ability extra levels; its row on the keeper screen says "A main ability pick is waiting". Every hit an ability deals itself takes its level: its swing or its projectiles, a hit of a thing it placed (a totem's shot, a bloom's burst) and an op's hit that names it. A share of an earlier hit (an ailment's ticks, a repeat, an echo) is a share of a hit that already carried the level, so it is not multiplied again. Minions do not take their summoner's level in this item. The abilities that deal no damage of their own gain nothing yet from a level; they can still be picked. Your picks are saved with your character.

## Render

Render turns one spare copy into 1 white shard (the `RenderWhite` table value). The copy is gone; the first copy stays, so with one copy of a soul Render is refused, as Stage is. A cosmetic soul renders to nothing: it is refused. There is no way back: no shard or bench verb ever makes a soul.

## The station (CT-5.2)

The soul-keeper stands in the town as a small stone pillar with a glowing orb over it and "Soul-keeper" written above, in the graveyard in the town's south-east corner, inside the ruin walls east of the South road (moved there by hand on 2026-11-17; before that it stood on the square beside the shrine and the gem bench). Walk up and click the block, or press the pick-up key beside it, and two panels open on the right of the screen as they do at the bench: your inventory, and the keeper's ledger beside it. The block lights under the cursor as the bench does. Walk more than four metres away, press Escape, or close either panel, and both close.

The keeper's verbs run at the keeper. A button on the screen, yours or a joiner's, is refused with "Stand at the soul-keeper to do that." unless your character stands within the block's reach (the same reach that opens the screen, with a step of slack for a joiner the server sees a little late); nothing is spent on a refusal. That holds however the screen was opened, so a screen opened by the console away from the block shows the ledger and refuses every verb. The host's own console verbs (`Slime.Keeper`) work from anywhere, as the bench's do.

## The screen

One row a soul you hold, slotted souls first: its orb, its name, how many copies you hold ("x 6"), five pips that fill to its stage, and no quirk line until quirks exist (E-5.11; a "No quirk yet" line stood there for a day and Jon had it removed on 2026-11-09 as confusing). The essence line each row carried went with E-5.29. The human, a body alone, is shown dim and cannot be chosen: it has no stage and nothing to render. A staged soul's row also names its main ability and level ("Main: Venom Rake, level 5"), or says that a pick is waiting. Under the souls, in the same scrolling list, **ABILITIES** lists the chosen soul's abilities (never the basic attack), each with its level and two buttons, **Main** and **Secondary**; the one an ability holds is lit, a button that cannot act is greyed with its reason as its tooltip (the stage is too low; it already holds the other pick). A pick comes back as a card ("CREEPY: MAIN ABILITY", the ability's name, "Picking again is free."). Click a row to choose that soul; the two buttons under the list then read what they will do and what it costs, before you confirm:

- **Stage II · 1 spare Creepy soul** buys the next stage with spare souls of that kind.
- **Render · 1 spare Creepy soul · +1 white shard** turns one spare into a white shard (never the other way).

A button that cannot act is greyed with its reason under it: "No spare soul", "Stage V, the most it can". A click sends the request; the answer comes back as a card under the buttons, where it covers no row ("CREEPY STAGE II" with what it consumed and what it gives; "RENDERED: +1 white shard" with the spare it consumed) and the row, the inventory's orb badge and the shard counts at the foot redraw from what the server holds. The keeper validates every click as it validates the console verbs: a button never promises what the keeper denies, and a request the server refuses spends nothing.

## Essence after E-5.29

Nothing makes essence any more and nothing spends it. Essence a character already holds stays in its `Essence_<SoulKey>` count rows and in the save, inert; `Slime.ShowKeeper` still lists it. GDD 4.4 had essence pay for a quirk's Rethread (E-5.11), the soul reagents (E-5.12), the lair altar that wakes a lair's Elite early (E-5.27; its `AltarEssence` and `AltarCooldownSeconds` rows stay in the table, unread) and a chosen ability card; those now wait on Jon's pick of a cost and none is built. No path returns copies, fragments or essence; `scripts/check_crafting_economy.py` asserts the flow is acyclic every time it runs.

## For testers

At the station: `Slime.Teleport Town`, walk to the graveyard in the south-east corner (the pillar stands at about (1120, 2400)) and click it, or `Slime.KeeperPoint click` from within sight of it; `Slime.Keeper.Screen show` logs the rows, `Slime.Keeper.Screen select Corpse` chooses a row, `Slime.Keeper.Screen stage|render` presses a button (through the same request a click sends, so a greyed button's verb is refused by the server with its `Craft: ... refused` line), `Slime.Keeper.Screen open|close` opens it anywhere (with no block behind it, it never closes for distance, and away from the block its verbs are refused `Not At Keeper`). A joiner opens its own keeper screen at the same block and its clicks go through the server, which refuses them away from the block.

Ability levels (E-2.301): `Slime.PickMain <ability>` and `Slime.PickSecondary <ability>` pick for the soul the ability belongs to (its row name, `KitCreepyVenomRake`, or its name without spaces among the worn soul's, `VenomRake`); a joiner's goes over the wire and needs the keeper in reach. `Slime.ShowAbilityLevels` logs every held soul's abilities with their level, floor, stage levels, cap, what is banked and the damage factor. Each hit at a level over 1 logs `Hit: <pawn>'s <ability> is ability level N (floor F, stage levels B, cap C): its base xF` just before its `Hit:` line. `Slime.AbilityLevels 0` turns the hit factor off.

Host only, without the screen: `Slime.AddSoulCopy DA_Soul_Creepy 9` puts nine spare Creepies in the bag; `Slime.Keeper stage Creepy` buys the next stage (the log line `Craft: <pawn> staged DA_Soul_Creepy to III (3) for 2 spare copies: +15 % ...`), `Slime.Keeper render Creepy` renders one spare (`Craft: ... rendered one spare DA_Soul_Creepy into 1 white shard(s) (now 1)`). `Slime.ShowKeeper` prints every soul's copies, spares, stage, essence, quirk and fed regions with the table's values. A refusal is one `Craft: ... keeper <verb> refused: <reason>` line and a notice on the HUD. The `I` screen shows the stage on the orb. Save and reload: the copies, the stage and the shard all come back (`Slime.SaveAll`, then relaunch).

Where the growth half reads from (checked 2026-11-09 by a read of every soul asset): the stage multiplies the growth a soul contributes today, which is the two rows on the soul asset, `HealthGrowth` and `ManaGrowth` on `DA_Soul_*`; `scripts/editor/soul_webs.py` writes them from `healthGrowth` / `manaGrowth` in `Data/Authoring/Souls/bodies.json`, and a body asset (`DA_Body_*`, E-2.221) carries no growth of its own. Eight souls have values (the Ogre, Golem and Treant 14 health and 3 mana a level, the Oni 11 and 5, the Gargoyle 12 and 4, the Vetala 9 and 5, the Vampire 8 and 6, the Eloko 7 and 6); the Creepy and the Corpse have no `healthGrowth` key in `bodies.json` and read 0 on the asset, so a stage changes no vital of theirs until values are set (the D-2.2 memo's placeholder for the Creepy is 5 and 2, never entered). Seen in a game: an Ogre at level 10 reads 316 health, 122 mana at stage 0 and 334.9, 126.05 at stage III, 15 % of its 14 x 9 and 3 x 9 growth share; a Creepy stays at 190 and 95 through stage III. The formula's log line (`<pawn>: level 10, MaxHealth ... primary stage 3 +15 %`) names what it multiplied; the move damage half is live for every soul.

## The screen's look after the UI audit (E-2.294, 2026-11-12)

The soul list shows a scroll bar when it holds more rows than fit. A row that cannot be worked (a body alone, the cosmetic "Human") keeps its words readable in the theme's Locked colour; only its orb is faded, and its tooltip says why it is off (a click on it does nothing). The line under a verb that says why it is refused ("No spare soul") is red and one size larger. The panel ends 256 above the foot of the view, clear of the mana orb's label. Not yet seen in the running game.
