# The soul-keeper

*Built in roadmap items E-5.9, E-5.10 and CT-5.2's first half (2026-11-08 and 2026-11-09); the verbs folded to two, Stage and Render, in E-5.29 (2026-11-12, Jon's playtest note of 2026-11-10). Design rules: GDD 2.6 (stages), 2.8 (spare copies), 4.4 (the two currencies) and 6.3 (the station), decided 2026-11-08 on Jon's rulings; `docs/decisions/D-5.4.md` Part B as amended by its section 15 (shape A, the ledger). Nothing here is provisional. The station stands in the town as a grey block with its own screen; its body, a MetaHuman NPC, is CT-5.2's second half and is not built.*

## The idea

Souls are never lost ([Souls](soul-model.md)): the first copy of a soul you win is the soul itself, the unlock, and no verb anywhere spends it. Every copy past the first is a **spare copy**, and spares are a currency. They buy the permanent things on that soul: its tree regions, fed at a gate on the tree screen ([Soul webs](soul-webs.md), Feeding a region's gate), and its **stages**, bought at the soul-keeper. A spare can also be **rendered** into a white shard for the gem bench. The flow is one way, kill to bench: fragments assemble into a copy, a copy renders into a white shard, and nothing ever returns a copy, a fragment or essence. A duplicate soul is never worthless and never worth more than trash.

Until E-5.29 a spare rendered into **essence** of its soul's type, and essence bought the recurring things (Rethread, the lair altar, a chosen card) and white shards at an exchange. Jon's playtest note of 2026-11-10 folded that away: "for soul-keeper fold render and exchange into just exchange, crafts should be stage and render (where render exchanges 1 soul for 1 white shard)". No verb makes essence now and the Exchange is gone; essence already in a bag stays there and does nothing. What pays Rethread (E-5.11), the soul reagents (E-5.12) and the lair altar (E-5.27) is Jon's pick, not yet made.

## Stages

A soul has a stage, 0 to V. Each stage costs spare copies of that soul: one for I, one for II, two for III, two for IV, three for V, nine copies in all (placeholders in the `DT_SoulKeeper` table). Each stage adds 5 % (a table value) to that soul's own growth values, the health and mana it adds a level, and to the base damage of its own moves, and the stage is applied before any tree node or card, so a node that adds flat damage adds it on top of the staged base. The stage counts while the soul is worn: a stage III Creepy's moves hit 15 % harder than its rows say while you are the Creepy, and its growth share rides the same multiplier whether it is your primary or your secondary. The stage is kept on the soul's unlock and never lost; it is never summed into any score. It shows on the soul's orb as a roman numeral in gold at the orb's lower left, across from the level at the lower right, in the soul slots and on the bag cell. A cosmetic soul (the human) has no stage.

A stage also unlocks one dye for the soul's body and a glow for its orb (GDD 2.6); the dye is E-5.28 and is not built.

## Render

Render turns one spare copy into 1 white shard (the `RenderWhite` table value). The copy is gone; the first copy stays, so with one copy of a soul Render is refused, as Stage is. A cosmetic soul renders to nothing: it is refused. There is no way back: no shard or bench verb ever makes a soul.

## The station (CT-5.2)

The soul-keeper stands in the town as a grey block about chest high with "Soul-keeper" written over it, on the far side of the shrine from the gem bench (the shrine between them, so the two stations never read as one table). Walk up and click the block, or press the pick-up key beside it, and two panels open on the right of the screen as they do at the bench: your inventory, and the keeper's ledger beside it. The block lights under the cursor as the bench does. Walk more than four metres away, press Escape, or close either panel, and both close.

The keeper's verbs run at the keeper. A button on the screen, yours or a joiner's, is refused with "Stand at the soul-keeper to do that." unless your character stands within the block's reach (the same reach that opens the screen, with a step of slack for a joiner the server sees a little late); nothing is spent on a refusal. That holds however the screen was opened, so a screen opened by the console away from the block shows the ledger and refuses every verb. The host's own console verbs (`Slime.Keeper`) work from anywhere, as the bench's do.

## The screen

One row a soul you hold, slotted souls first: its orb, its name, how many copies you hold ("x 6"), five pips that fill to its stage, and no quirk line until quirks exist (E-5.11; a "No quirk yet" line stood there for a day and Jon had it removed on 2026-11-09 as confusing). The essence line each row carried went with E-5.29. The human, a body alone, is shown dim and cannot be chosen: it has no stage and nothing to render. Click a row to choose that soul; the two buttons under the list then read what they will do and what it costs, before you confirm:

- **Stage II · 1 spare Creepy soul** buys the next stage with spare souls of that kind.
- **Render · 1 spare Creepy soul · +1 white shard** turns one spare into a white shard (never the other way).

A button that cannot act is greyed with its reason under it: "No spare soul", "Stage V, the most it can". A click sends the request; the answer comes back as a card under the buttons, where it covers no row ("CREEPY STAGE II" with what it consumed and what it gives; "RENDERED: +1 white shard" with the spare it consumed) and the row, the inventory's orb badge and the shard counts at the foot redraw from what the server holds. The keeper validates every click as it validates the console verbs: a button never promises what the keeper denies, and a request the server refuses spends nothing.

## Essence after E-5.29

Nothing makes essence any more and nothing spends it. Essence a character already holds stays in its `Essence_<SoulKey>` count rows and in the save, inert; `Slime.ShowKeeper` still lists it. GDD 4.4 had essence pay for a quirk's Rethread (E-5.11), the soul reagents (E-5.12), the lair altar that wakes a lair's Elite early (E-5.27; its `AltarEssence` and `AltarCooldownSeconds` rows stay in the table, unread) and a chosen ability card; those now wait on Jon's pick of a cost and none is built. No path returns copies, fragments or essence; `scripts/check_crafting_economy.py` asserts the flow is acyclic every time it runs.

## For testers

At the station: `Slime.Teleport Town`, walk south past the shrine (the block stands at about (500, -850)) and click the block, or `Slime.KeeperPoint click` from within sight of it; `Slime.Keeper.Screen show` logs the rows, `Slime.Keeper.Screen select Corpse` chooses a row, `Slime.Keeper.Screen stage|render` presses a button (through the same request a click sends, so a greyed button's verb is refused by the server with its `Craft: ... refused` line), `Slime.Keeper.Screen open|close` opens it anywhere (with no block behind it, it never closes for distance, and away from the block its verbs are refused `Not At Keeper`). A joiner opens its own keeper screen at the same block and its clicks go through the server, which refuses them away from the block.

Host only, without the screen: `Slime.AddSoulCopy DA_Soul_Creepy 9` puts nine spare Creepies in the bag; `Slime.Keeper stage Creepy` buys the next stage (the log line `Craft: <pawn> staged DA_Soul_Creepy to III (3) for 2 spare copies: +15 % ...`), `Slime.Keeper render Creepy` renders one spare (`Craft: ... rendered one spare DA_Soul_Creepy into 1 white shard(s) (now 1)`). `Slime.ShowKeeper` prints every soul's copies, spares, stage, essence, quirk and fed regions with the table's values. A refusal is one `Craft: ... keeper <verb> refused: <reason>` line and a notice on the HUD. The `I` screen shows the stage on the orb. Save and reload: the copies, the stage and the shard all come back (`Slime.SaveAll`, then relaunch).

Where the growth half reads from (checked 2026-11-09 by a read of every soul asset): the stage multiplies the growth a soul contributes today, which is the two rows on the soul asset, `HealthGrowth` and `ManaGrowth` on `DA_Soul_*`; `scripts/editor/soul_webs.py` writes them from `healthGrowth` / `manaGrowth` in `Data/Authoring/Souls/bodies.json`, and a body asset (`DA_Body_*`, E-2.221) carries no growth of its own. Eight souls have values (the Ogre, Golem and Treant 14 health and 3 mana a level, the Oni 11 and 5, the Gargoyle 12 and 4, the Vetala 9 and 5, the Vampire 8 and 6, the Eloko 7 and 6); the Creepy and the Corpse have no `healthGrowth` key in `bodies.json` and read 0 on the asset, so a stage changes no vital of theirs until values are set (the D-2.2 memo's placeholder for the Creepy is 5 and 2, never entered). Seen in a game: an Ogre at level 10 reads 316 health, 122 mana at stage 0 and 334.9, 126.05 at stage III, 15 % of its 14 x 9 and 3 x 9 growth share; a Creepy stays at 190 and 95 through stage III. The formula's log line (`<pawn>: level 10, MaxHealth ... primary stage 3 +15 %`) names what it multiplied; the move damage half is live for every soul.

## For engineers

`Source/SandboxARPG/Crafting/SoulKeeperRules.*` reads `DT_SoulKeeper` by row name (`StageCost1` to `StageCost5`, `StageFactor`, `StageCap`, `RenderWhite`, and the unread `AltarEssence`, `AltarCooldownSeconds`) through a config path in `DefaultGame.ini`, the `UCharacterRules` pattern; the CSV is `Data/DT_SoulKeeper.csv`, the asset was made by `scripts/editor/make_soul_keeper_table.py`. The pure rules are on `FCraftingRules` (`Crafting/CraftingRules.*`): `StageMultiplier`, `StageNumeral`, `EvaluateStage`, `EvaluateRender`, and `IsEssenceId`, `EssenceSoulKey` beside S1's `EssenceId` for the essence still held. The verbs are `UCraftingComponent::RequestKeeper(EKeeperOp, FName Soul)` on any machine, `ServerRequestKeeper` (the `Craft` rate row of `ServerRules.cpp`), and `Keeper` on the authority dispatching to `Stage` and `Render`, each refusing with an `ECraftRefusal` (`NotOwned`, `CosmeticSoul`, `StageAtCap`, `NoSpareCopy`; `NotEnoughEssence` stays in the enum for its wire value, refused by nothing) and telling the owner through `ClientCraftRefused` or `ClientCraftNotice` (`Staged`, `Rendered`; never the bench's card). `EKeeperOp` lost its last entry, `Exchange`, in E-5.29, so an old client's 2 is refused `UnknownOp`. Copies: `USoulComponent::GetCopies`, `GetSpareCopies` (the one spare rule the gates and the keeper share) and `ConsumeSoulCopies`. The stage: `UProgressionComponent::GetSoulStage` and `SetSoulStage` (E-5.9); the growth multiplier in `RecomputeVitals`; the move multiplier in `USoulWebComponent::ResolveMove`, on `Row.FlatDamage` right after the table row is copied and before any node override, the cache keyed on the worn soul's stage (`CachedStage`). Persistence: the record's `Keeper` section (E-5.9) carries the stage, the `Crafting` section any essence still held as `Essence_<SoulKey>` rows, and `Souls.Copies` (E-5.10) the copy count per unlock so spares survive a load; `FCharacterRecordSouls::CopiesOf` and `GetRestorePlan`. The badge: `UI/SoulSlotWidget.cpp` (`StageText`) and `UI/SoulItemCell.cpp` (`StageBadge`), refreshed on `OnSoulKeeperChanged`. Dev commands: `Crafting/KeeperConsoleCommands.cpp`. Tests: `SandboxARPG.Crafting.Keeper.*` (three) and `SandboxARPG.Server.Records.CopiesRoundTrip`, `CopiesOldRecordReadsOne`. The economy: `scripts/check_crafting_economy.py`, the soul path.

The station (CT-5.2): `Zones/SoulKeeperActor.*` is the bench's shape (`AGemBenchActor`): a placed actor with a BlockAll `Marker`, a `UGemPickupWidget` label, the bench's hover rim material laid on by `SetHovered` from `AMonsterHUD::CheckDropHover`, `ReachRadius` 250 with `IsInReach` through the bench's own `AGemBenchActor::EvaluateInReach` so one reach rule serves both stations, and `FindInReach`. It is placed once on `Lvl_Slice_WP` by `scripts/editor/place_soul_keeper.py` (label `SoulKeeper_Town`, folder Town, never spatially loaded; a new external actor file), never spawned, so every machine has it and nothing replicates. The click: `AMonsterCharacter::FindKeeperUnderCursor` (one Visibility trace, a second finder beside `FindBenchUnderCursor`), `BeginKeeperWalk` / `UpdateKeeperWalk` (`ELeftButtonMode::Keeper`, the bench walk's path and arrival), and `FindKeeperInReach` for the pick-up key, each right after the bench's own check. The screen: `UI/SoulKeeperScreen.*`, a `UHudScreen` on the bench's panel (`PanelWidth` 440, the inventory docked by `AMonsterHUD::ToggleKeeper` with `SetDockOffset`); the pure rules `BuildRows` (spares = copies - 1, a cosmetic soul dim, no copies no row), `DescribeStage` / `DescribeRender` (the label with the cost and the greyed reason, on `FCraftingRules::EvaluateStage` and `EvaluateRender`), `ReasonText`, `ShouldClose` (`KeeperCloseRange` 400 on a 0.5 s timer) and `DescribeOutcome`; a click is `UCraftingComponent::RequestKeeperAtStation`, which on the authority refuses `ECraftRefusal::NotAtKeeper` unless `IsAtKeeper` (an `ASoulKeeperActor::FindInReach` with the bench's `BenchReachSlack`), the bench's `RequestBenchCraft` / `IsAtBench` shape, and `ServerRequestKeeper` makes the same check for every verb over the wire while the dev `RequestKeeper` skips it on the host; the outcome card is a child of the panel's column under the verbs, not a canvas child over the rows; `UCraftingComponent::ClientCraftNotice` hands a keeper notice to the open keeper screen's card (`ShowOutcome`) before the HUD line; the rows redraw on `OnSoulSlotsChanged`, `OnSoulKeeperChanged` and `OnCountsChanged`. `AMonsterHUD` treats the keeper as it treats the bench (`IsKeeperOpen`, the close order, the click-free test, the inventory going with it). Dev: `Slime.Keeper.Screen` (`UI/HudConsoleCommands.cpp`) and `Slime.KeeperPoint` (`Crafting/KeeperConsoleCommands.cpp`). Tests: `SandboxARPG.UI.SoulKeeper.*` (three: the rows, the buttons and the outcome words, open and close) and `SandboxARPG.Crafting.Keeper.VerbsNeedTheKeeperInReach` (the station gate's distances and words).

## The screen's look after the UI audit (E-2.294, 2026-11-12)

The soul list shows a scroll bar when it holds more rows than fit.
