# The soul-keeper

*Built in roadmap items E-5.9, E-5.10 and CT-5.2's first half (2026-11-08 and 2026-11-09). Design rules: GDD 2.6 (stages), 2.8 (spare copies), 4.4 (the two currencies) and 6.3 (the station), decided 2026-11-08 on Jon's rulings; `docs/decisions/D-5.4.md` Part B as amended by its section 15 (shape A, the ledger). Nothing here is provisional. The station stands in the town as a grey block with its own screen; its body, a MetaHuman NPC, is CT-5.2's second half and is not built.*

## The idea

Souls are never lost ([Souls](soul-model.md)): the first copy of a soul you win is the soul itself, the unlock, and no verb anywhere spends it. Every copy past the first is a **spare copy**, and spares are a currency. They buy the permanent things on that soul: its tree regions, fed at a gate on the tree screen ([Soul webs](soul-webs.md), Feeding a region's gate), and its **stages**, bought at the soul-keeper. A spare can also be **rendered** into **essence** of its soul's type, and essence buys the recurring things: the quirk's Rethread and the lair altar when they exist, a chosen ability card once cards exist, and, as the floor, white shards for the gem bench. The flow is one way, kill to bench: fragments assemble into a copy, a copy renders into essence, essence is spent, and nothing ever returns a copy, a fragment or essence. A duplicate soul is never worthless and never worth more than trash.

## Stages

A soul has a stage, 0 to V. Each stage costs spare copies of that soul: one for I, one for II, two for III, two for IV, three for V, nine copies in all (placeholders in the `DT_SoulKeeper` table). Each stage adds 5 % (a table value) to that soul's own growth values, the health and mana it adds a level, and to the base damage of its own moves, and the stage is applied before any tree node or card, so a node that adds flat damage adds it on top of the staged base. The stage counts while the soul is worn: a stage III Creepy's moves hit 15 % harder than its rows say while you are the Creepy, and its growth share rides the same multiplier whether it is your primary or your secondary. The stage is kept on the soul's unlock and never lost; it is never summed into any score. It shows on the soul's orb as a roman numeral in gold at the orb's lower left, across from the level at the lower right, in the soul slots and on the bag cell. A cosmetic soul (the human) has no stage.

A stage also unlocks one dye for the soul's body and a glow for its orb (GDD 2.6); the dye is E-5.28 and is not built.

## Render

Render turns one spare copy into 3 essence of that soul's type (a table value). The copy is gone; the first copy stays, so with one copy of a soul Render is refused, as Stage is. Essence is counted beside your shards, one count per soul type, and is yours alone to see. A cosmetic soul renders to nothing: it is refused.

## Exchange

Exchange spends 3 essence (a table value) for 1 white shard. You may name the type to spend, or name none and the keeper takes from your largest essence stacks first, the biggest pile paying before the small ones are touched. With fewer than 3 essence in all it is refused. There is no exchange the other way: no shard, reagent or bench verb ever makes essence.

## The station (CT-5.2)

The soul-keeper stands in the town as a grey block about chest high with "Soul-keeper" written over it, on the far side of the shrine from the gem bench (the shrine between them, so the two stations never read as one table). Walk up and click the block, or press the pick-up key beside it, and two panels open on the right of the screen as they do at the bench: your inventory, and the keeper's ledger beside it. The block lights under the cursor as the bench does. Walk more than four metres away, press Escape, or close either panel, and both close.

The keeper's verbs run at the keeper. A button on the screen, yours or a joiner's, is refused with "Stand at the soul-keeper to do that." unless your character stands within the block's reach (the same reach that opens the screen, with a step of slack for a joiner the server sees a little late); nothing is spent on a refusal. That holds however the screen was opened, so a screen opened by the console away from the block shows the ledger and refuses every verb. The host's own console verbs (`Slime.Keeper`) work from anywhere, as the bench's do.

## The screen

One row a soul you hold, slotted souls first: its orb, its name, how many copies you hold ("x 6"), five pips that fill to its stage, the quirk line ("No quirk yet" until quirks exist, E-5.11) and how much essence of its kind you hold. The human, a body alone, is shown dim and cannot be chosen: it has no stage and no essence. Click a row to choose that soul; the three buttons under the list then read what they will do and what it costs, before you confirm:

- **Stage II · 1 spare Creepy soul** buys the next stage with spare souls of that kind.
- **Render · 1 spare Creepy soul · +3 essence** turns one spare into essence of its kind.
- **Exchange · 3 essence · +1 white shard** spends that soul's essence for a white shard (never the other way).

A button that cannot act is greyed with its reason under it: "No spare soul", "Stage V, the most it can", "Not enough essence". A click sends the request; the answer comes back as a card under the buttons, where it covers no row ("CREEPY STAGE II" with what it consumed and what it gives; "RENDERED: +3 Creepy essence"; "EXCHANGED: +1 white shard") and the row, the inventory's orb badge and the shard counts at the foot redraw from what the server holds. The keeper validates every click as it validates the console verbs: a button never promises what the keeper denies, and a request the server refuses spends nothing.

## What essence is for, and never for

Essence buys the recurring things of GDD 4.4: a quirk's Rethread (E-5.11), the lair altar that wakes a lair's Elite early (E-5.27, 6 essence a wake and one wake a lair an hour as placeholders, rows already in the table), a chosen ability card once the card layer exists, and white shards at the floor rate. Essence never buys a gem, an Etching Needle, an Expanding Lattice or a Polishing Wheel, and no path returns copies, fragments or essence from it; `scripts/check_crafting_economy.py` asserts the flow is acyclic every time it runs.

## For testers

At the station: `Slime.Teleport Town`, walk south past the shrine (the block stands at about (500, -850)) and click the block, or `Slime.KeeperPoint click` from within sight of it; `Slime.Keeper.Screen show` logs the rows, `Slime.Keeper.Screen select Corpse` chooses a row, `Slime.Keeper.Screen stage|render|exchange` presses a button (through the same request a click sends, so a greyed button's verb is refused by the server with its `Craft: ... refused` line), `Slime.Keeper.Screen open|close` opens it anywhere (with no block behind it, it never closes for distance, and away from the block its verbs are refused `Not At Keeper`). A joiner opens its own keeper screen at the same block and its clicks go through the server, which refuses them away from the block.

Host only, without the screen: `Slime.AddSoulCopy DA_Soul_Creepy 9` puts nine spare Creepies in the bag; `Slime.Keeper stage Creepy` buys the next stage (the log line `Craft: <pawn> staged DA_Soul_Creepy to III (3) for 2 spare copies: +15 % ...`), `Slime.Keeper render Creepy` renders one spare (`Craft: ... rendered one spare DA_Soul_Creepy into 3 Essence_Creepy (now 3)`), `Slime.Keeper exchange Creepy` spends 3 Creepy essence for a white shard, `Slime.Keeper exchange` with no soul spends the largest stacks. `Slime.ShowKeeper` prints every soul's copies, spares, stage, essence, quirk and fed regions with the table's values. A refusal is one `Craft: ... keeper <verb> refused: <reason>` line and a notice on the HUD. The `I` screen shows the stage on the orb. Save and reload: the copies, the stage, the essence and the shard all come back (`Slime.SaveAll`, then relaunch).

Where the growth half reads from (checked 2026-11-09 by a read of every soul asset): the stage multiplies the growth a soul contributes today, which is the two rows on the soul asset, `HealthGrowth` and `ManaGrowth` on `DA_Soul_*`; `scripts/editor/soul_webs.py` writes them from `healthGrowth` / `manaGrowth` in `Data/Authoring/Souls/bodies.json`, and a body asset (`DA_Body_*`, E-2.221) carries no growth of its own. Eight souls have values (the Ogre, Golem and Treant 14 health and 3 mana a level, the Oni 11 and 5, the Gargoyle 12 and 4, the Vetala 9 and 5, the Vampire 8 and 6, the Eloko 7 and 6); the Creepy and the Corpse have no `healthGrowth` key in `bodies.json` and read 0 on the asset, so a stage changes no vital of theirs until values are set (the D-2.2 memo's placeholder for the Creepy is 5 and 2, never entered). Seen in a game: an Ogre at level 10 reads 316 health, 122 mana at stage 0 and 334.9, 126.05 at stage III, 15 % of its 14 x 9 and 3 x 9 growth share; a Creepy stays at 190 and 95 through stage III. The formula's log line (`<pawn>: level 10, MaxHealth ... primary stage 3 +15 %`) names what it multiplied; the move damage half is live for every soul.

## For engineers

`Source/SandboxARPG/Crafting/SoulKeeperRules.*` reads `DT_SoulKeeper` by row name (`StageCost1` to `StageCost5`, `StageFactor`, `StageCap`, `RenderEssence`, `ExchangeEssencePerWhite`, `AltarEssence`, `AltarCooldownSeconds`) through a config path in `DefaultGame.ini`, the `UCharacterRules` pattern; the CSV is `Data/DT_SoulKeeper.csv`, the asset was made by `scripts/editor/make_soul_keeper_table.py`. The pure rules are on `FCraftingRules` (`Crafting/CraftingRules.*`): `StageMultiplier`, `StageNumeral`, `EvaluateStage`, `EvaluateRender`, `PlanExchange`, `IsEssenceId`, `EssenceSoulKey`, beside S1's `EssenceId`. The verbs are `UCraftingComponent::RequestKeeper(EKeeperOp, FName Soul)` on any machine, `ServerRequestKeeper` (the `Craft` rate row of `ServerRules.cpp`), and `Keeper` on the authority dispatching to `Stage`, `Render` and `Exchange`, each refusing with an `ECraftRefusal` (`NotOwned`, `CosmeticSoul`, `StageAtCap`, `NoSpareCopy`, `NotEnoughEssence`) and telling the owner through `ClientCraftRefused` or `ClientCraftNotice` (`Staged`, `Rendered`, `Exchanged`; never the bench's card). Copies: `USoulComponent::GetCopies`, `GetSpareCopies` (the one spare rule the gates and the keeper share) and `ConsumeSoulCopies`. The stage: `UProgressionComponent::GetSoulStage` and `SetSoulStage` (E-5.9); the growth multiplier in `RecomputeVitals`; the move multiplier in `USoulWebComponent::ResolveMove`, on `Row.FlatDamage` right after the table row is copied and before any node override, the cache keyed on the worn soul's stage (`CachedStage`). Persistence: the record's `Keeper` section (E-5.9) carries the stage, the `Crafting` section the essence as `Essence_<SoulKey>` rows, and `Souls.Copies` (E-5.10) the copy count per unlock so spares survive a load; `FCharacterRecordSouls::CopiesOf` and `GetRestorePlan`. The badge: `UI/SoulSlotWidget.cpp` (`StageText`) and `UI/SoulItemCell.cpp` (`StageBadge`), refreshed on `OnSoulKeeperChanged`. Dev commands: `Crafting/KeeperConsoleCommands.cpp`. Tests: `SandboxARPG.Crafting.Keeper.*` (three) and `SandboxARPG.Server.Records.CopiesRoundTrip`, `CopiesOldRecordReadsOne`. The economy: `scripts/check_crafting_economy.py`, the soul path.

The station (CT-5.2): `Zones/SoulKeeperActor.*` is the bench's shape (`AGemBenchActor`): a placed actor with a BlockAll `Marker`, a `UGemPickupWidget` label, the bench's hover rim material laid on by `SetHovered` from `AMonsterHUD::CheckDropHover`, `ReachRadius` 250 with `IsInReach` through the bench's own `AGemBenchActor::EvaluateInReach` so one reach rule serves both stations, and `FindInReach`.
